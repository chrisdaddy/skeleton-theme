FAQ

Questions before you install.

Anything else, ask in the Discord. A human answers within the day.

Is this just Dawn with new CSS?

No. Blueprint is original code, not derived from Dawn or any other theme, built to Shopify's Online Store 2.0 standards. It includes 8 templates, 70+ section layouts, 30+ product blocks, the bundle and subscription blocks, the cart drawer, three preset systems, the pre-landers and the MCP server.

Will my customizations survive updates?

Yes. Your content lives in Shopify JSON templates and section settings, not in theme code. The updater (npx @blueprint/mcp update) swaps the theme files and leaves your settings in place.

What does the AI kit actually do?

It is an MCP server plus a prompt library. Connect it to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini or any MCP-capable assistant and it can read your products, reviews, preset and the section library, then write pages, blocks, copy and image briefs into a draft theme. You review in the customizer and publish. It is included with every license and uses your own AI subscription.

Will it work with my apps?

Every product surface accepts app blocks, and the cart drawer refreshes when an app adds to the cart, so anything that injects via app blocks or the theme app extension works without edits. Shopify Subscriptions, Recharge, Judge.me, Okendo, Loox, Klaviyo, Postscript, Rebuy, Gorgias and Triple Whale are on the compatibility checklist we run before each minor release.

Do I lose SEO when I switch themes?

No. URLs, products, collections and metafields are store data, not theme data. We keep the heading structure and add proper schema for products and reviews. The speed gain usually helps rankings.

How long does the switch take?

DIY: most people are live in a weekend. Done-for-you: seven days from the intake form, including one revision round.

What exactly is in the done-for-you setup?

Install, preset tuned to your brand, up to three product pages migrated with bundle tiers and subscription set up, the cart drawer configured, one advertorial and one listicle written from your reviews, speed and mobile QA, a Loom walkthrough. Extra product pages are $150 each.

What is the difference between the presets?

Clinical, Editorial and Bold are colour, type, radius and spacing systems applied to the same sections. Switch presets in theme settings and every page restyles. You can also set every value yourself.

Refunds?

14 days on the theme, no questions. The setup fee is refundable until we start the build. Price goes up with each major release; buy once and every v1 update is yours.
