# Licences and the Blueprint Portal

Each Blueprint licence covers **one store**. The theme itself never checks your licence, so your storefront can't be switched off by it. The licence matters for:
- downloads;
- updates (`blueprint update`);
- support.

## Your keys

After you buy, sign in to the **Blueprint Portal** with the email address on your order. We email a one-time sign-in link; there's no password.

The Portal shows:
- **Your keys:** one per licence (five for an agency pack), and the store each one is used on.
- **Downloads:** the latest Blueprint zip. Links last 15 minutes; sign in again for a new one.
- **The changelog** for the latest version.

## Activating a key

A key is tied to a store the first time it's used, by the updater or the AI kit. The store is identified by its `*.myshopify.com` address.

## Moving a key to another store

Rebranding, or handing a store to a client? In the Portal, click **Move to another store** next to the key. The next store that uses the key gets it.

You can move a key **3 times in 30 days**. Contact support if you need more.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled from your order. A refunded licence's key stops working for downloads and updates; the theme already installed keeps working.

## Agency pack

The agency pack gives you five keys, each for one store. Extra licences can be added at any time.
