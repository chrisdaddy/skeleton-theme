name: shot-list-from-products
title: Shot list from products
description: A photo shot list for the store's products and sections.
---
Write a photo shot list for this store.

1. `read_products` (all pages) and `list_sections`. `read_template` the home and product templates to see which image slots are used.
2. Per product: a hero on plain background (square, 1200 × 1200), 3 detail or lifestyle shots, and a scale shot. Per section with an image: what it needs, the aspect ratio and the safe area.
3. Group shots by set-up so a photographer can shoot them in one session. Name files so the merchant can upload them in Content → Files.

This prompt changes nothing in the store.
